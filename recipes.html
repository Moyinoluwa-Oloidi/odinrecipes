NIGERIAN CLASSIC

Jollof Rice

Nigerian Jollof Rice is a rich and flavourful rice dish cooked in a delicious blend of tomatoes, peppers, onions and aromatic spices. It is a favourite at family gatherings and celebrations.

⏱ Prep Time

20 mins

🔥 Cooking Time

45 mins

🕐 Total Time

1 hr 5 mins

🍽 Servings

6 people

Ingredients

  • 3 cups long-grain rice
  • Fresh tomatoes
  • Red bell peppers
  • Scotch bonnet peppers
  • Onions
  • Tomato paste
  • Vegetable oil
  • Chicken stock
  • Seasoning cubes
  • Salt and spices

Directions

  1. Wash and prepare the rice.
  2. Blend the tomatoes, peppers and onions into a smooth mixture.
  3. Heat vegetable oil and fry the tomato paste for a few minutes.
  4. Add the blended pepper mixture and cook until the sauce thickens.
  5. Add stock, seasoning and rice.
  6. Cover and cook on low heat until the rice becomes tender.
  7. Fluff the rice and serve hot.

NIGERIAN FAVOURITE

Fried Plantain (Dodo)

Fried plantain, popularly known as Dodo, is made by frying ripe plantain slices until they become beautifully golden and slightly crispy.

⏱ Prep Time

10 mins

🔥 Cooking Time

10 mins

🕐 Total Time

20 mins

🍽 Servings

4 people

Ingredients

  • 4 ripe plantains
  • Vegetable oil
  • Salt

Directions

  1. Peel the ripe plantains.
  2. Slice them into your preferred shape.
  3. Sprinkle lightly with salt.
  4. Heat vegetable oil in a frying pan.
  5. Fry the plantain slices until golden brown on both sides.
  6. Remove and drain excess oil before serving.

TRADITIONAL DISH

Amala & Ewedu

Amala and Ewedu is a beloved Nigerian combination. Soft amala is traditionally served with smooth ewedu soup and can be enjoyed with a rich stew.

⏱ Prep Time

15 mins

🔥 Cooking Time

25 mins

🕐 Total Time

40 mins

🍽 Servings

4 people

Ingredients

  • Amala flour
  • Ewedu leaves
  • Water
  • Locust beans
  • Salt
  • Potash, if traditionally used
  • Optional stew

Directions

  1. Wash the ewedu leaves thoroughly.
  2. Cook the leaves until soft.
  3. Blend or mash the leaves until smooth.
  4. Add locust beans and season lightly.
  5. Prepare the amala flour with hot water while stirring continuously.
  6. Continue stirring until the amala becomes smooth and firm.
  7. Serve the amala with ewedu and stew.

NIGERIAN CLASSIC

Pounded Yam & Egusi Soup

Soft, smooth pounded yam served alongside rich egusi soup made with ground melon seeds, vegetables, seasoning and a choice of meat or fish.

⏱ Prep Time

20 mins

🔥 Cooking Time

50 mins

🕐 Total Time

1 hr 10 mins

🍽 Servings

6 people

Ingredients

  • Yam
  • Ground egusi
  • Leafy vegetables
  • Meat or fish
  • Palm oil
  • Peppers
  • Onions
  • Seasoning
  • Salt

Directions

  1. Peel and wash the yam.
  2. Cut the yam into pieces and boil until soft.
  3. Pound the cooked yam until smooth and stretchy.
  4. Prepare the egusi soup by frying the egusi with the required ingredients.
  5. Add vegetables, meat or fish and seasonings.
  6. Cook until the soup is well combined.
  7. Serve the pounded yam with hot egusi soup.

FAMILY FAVOURITE

Fried Rice & Chicken

Colourful Nigerian fried rice cooked with vegetables, seasonings and rice, served with deliciously seasoned chicken.

⏱ Prep Time

25 mins

🔥 Cooking Time

40 mins

🕐 Total Time

1 hr 5 mins

🍽 Servings

6 people

Ingredients

  • Long-grain rice
  • Carrots
  • Green peas
  • Green beans
  • Sweet corn
  • Onions
  • Vegetable oil
  • Seasoning
  • Chicken
  • Salt

Directions

  1. Wash and cook the rice until almost tender.
  2. Prepare and chop the vegetables.
  3. Stir-fry the vegetables with onions and seasoning.
  4. Add the cooked rice and mix thoroughly.
  5. Cook the chicken with seasoning and spices.
  6. Grill, bake or fry the chicken until fully cooked.
  7. Serve the fried rice with the chicken.

TRADITIONAL FAVOURITE

Moi Moi & Pap

Moi Moi is a soft steamed bean pudding made from blended beans and spices. It is traditionally enjoyed with smooth and warm Nigerian pap.

⏱ Prep Time

25 mins

🔥 Cooking Time

45 mins

🕐 Total Time

1 hr 10 mins

🍽 Servings

6 people

Ingredients

  • Beans
  • Red peppers
  • Onions
  • Vegetable oil
  • Seasoning
  • Salt
  • Pap or corn flour
  • Water

Directions

  1. Soak and remove the beans' outer skins.
  2. Blend the beans with peppers and onions.
  3. Add oil, seasoning and salt.
  4. Pour the mixture into suitable containers.
  5. Steam until the Moi Moi becomes firm and cooked.
  6. Prepare smooth pap separately.
  7. Serve the warm Moi Moi with pap.
